## Platform

## Stack

Next.js 16 (App Router) + React 19 + TypeScript, Vercel-ready. Chosen by the user from three offered options (Next.js / hand-written static HTML+CSS / multi-page), on the strength of an existing sibling project the user shipped for the same business type. Deploys from `https://github.com/Realm9758/phoenix-detailing`.

## Users

Vehicle owners in Cardiff and South Wales who bring a car to Scott's unit. Three groups are evidenced in the business's own reviews and Instagram:

- **Owners of high-end and enthusiast cars**: Teslas, a Mercedes-AMG E-Class, a BMW 8-Series, a Ferrari 360, wanting detailing, accredited ceramic coating, paint correction and window tints. One reviewer calls his car "my pride and joy".
- **Everyday owners** wanting a thorough full valet, sometimes fitted around ordinary life ("in between the morning and afternoon school run").
- **Lease returners and small fleet operators**: one reviewer had an end-of-lease deep clean ahead of returning a car; another says "I now bring my 'fleet' of cars to Scott".

The job is the same across all three: hand the car to someone who will be thorough with it, and get it back looking better than expected.

## Product Purpose

Phoenix Detailing Cardiff is an owner-operated vehicle detailing and valeting business run by Scott from a unit in Cardiff. It has an active Instagram presence and a body of strong Google reviews, but no website.

The site exists to convert someone who has heard of Phoenix (or found it searching for detailing in Cardiff) into an enquiry. Success is a phone call or a WhatsApp message to Scott. There is no other conversion event: no online booking, no form, no account, no e-commerce.

## Positioning

**Premium specialist** (confirmed by the owner). Detailing, accredited ceramic coating and paint correction lead; valeting is the entry point rather than the headline.

Two claims a neighbouring detailer could not truthfully copy, both evidenced:

- **Scott does the work himself, and talks you through it while he does it.** "He's brilliant and really nice to talk to while he does the work for you. Awesome small business." Six of eleven reviewers name Scott personally. This is not a team or a franchise.
- **The detail work is thorough enough to clear a lease-return inspection.** "Car returned with no issues or recharges due to small marks being buffed out as part of the service." That is a measurable financial outcome, not an adjective.

## Operating Context

The customer drops the car at the Cardiff unit and collects it when it is done. Confirmed by the owner; consistent with reviews ("I recently took my car to Scott at Phoenix Detailing in Cardiff") and with an Instagram caption about a customer collecting.

Booking happens by phone, WhatsApp or Instagram DM. The business's own banner carries Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp icons alongside the phone number. One reviewer describes a "quick and easy booking process"; another says Scott "went above and beyond to make it as easy as possible for me to get my car cleaned on my day off", so scheduling flexibility is part of the service.

The unit is an indoor bay: white panelled walls, ceiling strip lighting, a floor drainage channel, wall-mounted tools and a workbench. A branded Phoenix banner hangs on the back wall carrying the services and phone number.

## Product Principles

1. **Nothing is invented.** Every fact and every word traces to the business's own material or a customer's own words. Where a fact is missing it stays visibly missing, as an empty slot, rather than being filled with a plausible guess. A real business's reputation is on this page.
2. **The work is the proof.** Three real photographs of real cars in the real unit outrank any claim, badge or stock image. No stock photography, ever.
3. **One action.** Message or call Scott. Every section resolves to that; nothing competes with it.
4. **Premium specialist first.** Detailing, ceramic coating and correction lead. Valeting is the way in, not the headline.
5. **Scott, not "we".** Owner-operated is the differentiator, and it is what customers actually wrote about. The voice stays first-person and singular.
